# CV review and remodel — 4 October 2026

Scope: `cv/OKOnditi_CV_2026.docx` and `.pdf`, with the sources that generate them.
Standard applied: `assets/ai_documentation_rules.md` (rule numbers below refer to it).
Method: the CV was read in full, every claim was traced to its source or marked as unverified,
the text was rewritten, and the new files were compared with the old ones programmatically.

This revision was drafted and edited with Claude (Anthropic). Under rules 60–61 the content
still needs your review before external use; the "Verified / not verified" section below
separates what was checked from what was only generated.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **The PDF was out of date.** It showed 388 citations, i10 = 12, 74 manuscripts and 22
   journals from September 2026. The data file now has 403, 13, 76 and 24 (4 October 2026).
   Rebuilding picks the new figures up automatically.
2. **Two statements could not be supported** (rules 63–64): "Publication list … complete and
   verified against Crossref and the journal of record", and "verified via ORCID". Nothing in
   the pipeline checks completeness, and the list is not checked against each journal.
3. **Unsupported superlatives and value claims** (rules 15, 56): "extensive, long-running
   collaboration", "the largest single-site small mammal dataset from Kenya", "first
   comprehensive small mammal survey at several sites", "key data for comparative
   phylogeography", "strategic site selection".
4. **Causal wording for observational work** (rule 12): "pressures shape mammal diversity",
   "the influence of climatic and anthropogenic drivers", "resulting in a first-author publication".
5. **Internal inconsistencies in Field Research** (rules 10, 17): "All campaigns were joint
   NMK–KIZ surveys", but the last entry is a Field Museum-led team and has no specimen count,
   and the 5,974 total is the sum of the five counted campaigns only.
6. **Layout:** Publications began with a single entry at the foot of page 1, the body type was
   small (9.3 pt), and the key-figures strip gave no source or date for each figure.

## 4. Needs your confirmation

These are facts only you can settle. None was changed except where stated.

1. **Peer-review unit.** I could not reach ORCID (its robots.txt blocks automated access), so I
   cannot say whether the 76 records are 76 distinct manuscripts. The CV now says "records".
   If they are all distinct manuscripts, set `REVIEW_NOUN` and `REVIEW_LABEL` in
   `render_helpers.py` back to "manuscripts reviewed".
2. **Fieldwork data versus your own papers.**
   - Onditi et al. (2025, *Ecosphere*) states fieldwork ran "between September 6, 2015, and
     November 14, 2018". The CV has the last 2018 campaign ending 14 Sep 2018 (and a
     14 Nov **2019** end for Cherang'any). One of these is likely a typo.
   - Mount Kenya: the CV says 1,932 specimens; Musila et al. (2019, *Zool. Res.*) reports 1,905
     individuals from the same September–October 2015 survey (12,240 trap-nights). Different
     definitions (catalogued vs captured) would explain it, but the CV does not say so.
   - Tissue "in 95% ethanol" (CV) versus "absolute ethanol" for tissue and 70% ethanol for
     wet specimens (Musila et al. 2019).
   - The 2016 count includes bats, but no bat-capture method is listed.
   - The Jul–Aug 2015 entry has no count and its institutional framing is not stated.
   - If you want the removed value claims back, each needs a citation. Mweu et al. (2024)
     includes Loita Hills samples, so "specimens used in Mweu et al. (2024)" is defensible if
     they came from the January–February 2018 survey.
3. **Geographic scope.** Only one listed article (Liu et al. 2026) names Southeast Asia; most
   Asian-focused entries concern China and the Eastern Himalayas, plus one from Nepal. The CV
   says "China and Southeast Asia"; the website says "Southeast Asia" only.
4. **National Museums of Kenya** appears in the header affiliation line, but the CV describes no
   current NMK appointment. The website lists it as a current institution. Add the role if there is one.
5. **Post title.** The website says "Special Research Assistant (Postdoc)"; the CV omits "(Postdoc)".
6. **Dates.** PhD is 2018–2022, but the CAS–TWAS fellowship covers 2018–2021.
7. **Supervision.** The role (supervisor, co-supervisor, mentor) is not stated. The 2019–2022 MSc
   entries predate the July 2022 appointment. Samson Mabeya Ouru's entry has no topic or outcome.
8. **Currency.** "approx. US$33,300" implies about ¥7.21 per US$ with no rate or date. Add them or drop the figure.
9. **Year of record.** Syst. Biol. (2026) and Mammal Res. (2020) use the issue year, but Yu et al.
   (*Biodiversity and Conservation* 33(2): online 18 Dec 2023, so a 2024 issue) is shown as 2023, and the
   Frontiers entries use the online year (4 Jan 2023 for volume 10; 5 Jan 2022 for volume 9). Pick one rule.
10. **Name variants.** Prof. Kerbis Peterhans appears as "Peterhans, J. K." (3), "Kerbis Peterhans, J." (5)
    and "Kerbis Peterhans, J. C." (30); other co-authors vary between one and two initials. These follow
    each deposit, so I did not merge them.
11. **Referees and contact details.** Referee emails are removed from the public build, as you asked;
    titles (for example "Curator") were not verified. Your phone number and postal address remain in the PDF.
