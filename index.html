Research Focus

Three pillars of inquiry

Systematics & Phylogeography

Unravelling the evolutionary histories and biogeographic patterns of African small mammals using genomic and morphological data.

Explore →

Biodiversity Patterns

Investigating species richness, functional and phylogenetic diversity across elevational gradients, ecoregions and spatial scales.

Explore →

Conservation Biology

Evaluating protected area effectiveness, climate change vulnerability, and the role of anthropogenic factors in shaping mammal communities.
